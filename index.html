QF QuantFlex
In development

A derivatives pricing engine, built by hand and provably correct.

QuantFlex prices European vanillas, American options, path-dependent exotics and multi-asset baskets under Black–Scholes, Merton jump-diffusion and Heston stochastic volatility — via hand-rolled Monte Carlo and finite-difference PDE solvers, with Greeks computed by automatic differentiation.

No pricing library is used for the numerics. Every price is checked against an analytic anchor, and every Greek is derived three independent ways that must agree.

The public web app is still being built. Everything below is real output from the engine, produced at build time — not screenshots, and not hand-typed.

Greeks, derived three ways

This is the part that is hard to fake. Each Greek is computed by a hand-written reverse-mode AAD tape, independently by JAX, and again by central finite differences with common random numbers. All three must agree to a tolerance that was measured and then locked — never widened to make a test pass.

    Where it stands

    • Engine core — closed-form Black–Scholes, chunked Monte Carlo with standard errors, implied-vol solver, reproducible seeded draws
    • Greeks — hand-rolled AAD tape cross-verified against JAX and finite differences, with a payoff-smoothness registry
    • Model breadth — Merton jump-diffusion and Heston stochastic volatility, anchored on characteristic-function references
    • American options — Crank–Nicolson PDE with Rannacher startup, cross-checked against a Longstaff–Schwartz bias sandwich
    • Exotics & baskets — Asian, barrier and lookback payoffs, variance reduction, correlated multi-asset baskets
    • Public API & web app — pricing endpoints and the browser front end, in progress
    • Portfolios & risk — VaR/CVaR with component risk decomposition

    Not financial advice. QuantFlex is an engineering and mathematics project. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any instrument, and the market headlines are third-party links reproduced from public syndication feeds.
